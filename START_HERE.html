What's in this folder

APPROACH.md — the thinking: design, rollout phases gated on numbers, the eval strategy, risks named honestly.
demo/review.py — the whole prototype, ~250 lines, one file.
demo/playbook/mutual_nda.yaml — the positions, in plain language the legal team edits.
evals/eval.py — accuracy and false-green rate against a 21-clause golden set.

What this is not

Not legal advice, and it signs nothing. Every finding must quote the exact contract text it rests on, and the code verifies the quotes. A named human stays the accountable approver; autonomy is earned in measured phases, never assumed. The verdict that matters in the eval is the false-green rate: a problem waved through is the one failure mode this design treats as unacceptable.
