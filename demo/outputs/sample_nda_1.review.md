## Suggested redlines

**2. Definition of Confidential Information** *(precedents: P-004)*
> Information disclosed orally shall constitute Confidential Information only if identified as confidential at the time of disclosure and summarised in writing to the Receiving Party within thirty (30) days.

**5. Term and Survival** *(precedents: P-001)*
> The obligations of confidentiality shall survive expiry or termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure, save that obligations in respect of non-technical business information shall survive for five (5) years.
